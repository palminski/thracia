verb stem, … (連用中止)

Ending a clause on the verb stem (脱出し, やぶり) links it to the next clause, like "and". A written, formal alternative to the て-form.

〜とともにBunpro ↗

"Together with".

〜などBunpro ↗

"Such as, and the like".

〜ながらBunpro ↗

"While doing": two actions at the same time. Attaches to the verb stem.

〜ながら (contrast)Bunpro ↗

Here ながら means "although, even though", not "while".

A という BBunpro ↗

"B called A": names or defines the following noun.

〜で (copula て-form)Bunpro ↗

で here is the て-form of だ: "it is … and".

〜ているBunpro ↗

Ongoing action or resulting state ("is doing", "has done"). Past: 〜ていた.

〜てる／〜てんだ

Spoken contraction of 〜ている (やってる, 落ち着いてんだ = 落ち着いているのだ).

〜ておる／〜ております

Same as 〜ている. ております is humble; plain ておる sounds old or lordly.

〜とる／〜とり

Dialect / old-man contraction of 〜ておる (= 〜ている).

〜ていくBunpro ↗

Action moving away or continuing onward from now ("go on doing").

〜てくるBunpro ↗

Go do something and come back, or a change that has been coming up to now.

〜が、Bunpro ↗

Clause-final が = "but"; often just softens the lead-in.

〜ことBunpro ↗

Turns a clause into a noun ("the fact that …").

〜のはBunpro ↗

の turns the clause into a noun: "the fact that … is …".

〜のだ／〜のですBunpro ↗

Explanatory ending: states something as the explanation or as an emphatic fact.

〜んだ／〜んですBunpro ↗

Spoken form of 〜のだ: explanatory or emphatic; in questions, asks for an explanation.

〜なのBunpro ↗

Soft (often feminine) explanatory ending after a noun; = なのだ.

〜のだ (order)Bunpro ↗

Said to a subordinate, 〜のだ works as a firm order: "you are to …".

〜の／〜のよ (instruction)

Sentence-final の as a gentle but firm instruction (feminine).

〜せる／〜させるBunpro ↗

Causative: make or let someone do. はかせろ = "make (them) spit it out".

command formBunpro ↗

Plain imperative: 待て, 帰れ, 連れてこい, しろ／せよ. Blunt; used by superiors and rough speakers.

〜ようだ／〜ようですBunpro ↗

"It seems that …", judging from evidence.

〜れる／〜られる (passive)Bunpro ↗

Passive: "be …-ed". The doer is marked with に.

〜れる／〜られる (respect)Bunpro ↗

The passive form used as an honorific: なられた = "has become" (respectful).

potential formBunpro ↗

"Can do": 戦える, ゆける, 買えん. Negative = cannot.

stem + に行く／来るBunpro ↗

Purpose of going or coming: 助けに行く = "go to help".

〜と (quote)Bunpro ↗

と marks what is said, thought or heard.

〜ってBunpro ↗

Casual quoting particle (= と).

〜ども

Plural suffix that looks down on the group (山賊ども).

〜め

Insulting suffix on a name or noun (こぞうめ = "that brat").

〜そうだ (hearsay)Bunpro ↗

After a plain form: "I hear that …".

〜とかBunpro ↗

Vague hearsay: "… or something, I hear".

〜には及ばないBunpro ↗

"There is no need to …".

〜ぬ／〜ん (negative)

Old negative, = ない. できぬ = できない, かえん = 買えない. Typical of nobles, soldiers and old men.

〜でしょう／〜だろうBunpro ↗

Conjecture: "probably", or seeking agreement.

〜にしてはBunpro ↗

"For (someone like) …": better or worse than expected.

〜よう／〜おうBunpro ↗

Volitional: "let's …" or "I shall …".

〜とするか／〜としようBunpro ↗

"Let's …, shall we": deciding to do something now.

〜ましょうBunpro ↗

Polite "let's …" / "I shall …".

〜たらBunpro ↗

"When / if …" (once that has happened).

〜ばBunpro ↗

Conditional "if".

〜ばいいBunpro ↗

"It would be good if …; you should just …".

〜ならBunpro ↗

"If it is the case that …; if you mean …".

〜てもBunpro ↗

"Even if / even though".

〜てもいいBunpro ↗

"It's all right to …"; as a question, asks permission or acceptance.

〜なくても／〜なくたって

"Even without …-ing": 言われなくても = "without being told".

お + stem + くださいBunpro ↗

Respectful request: お待ちください = "please wait".

〜てくださいBunpro ↗

Polite request: "please do".

〜てくださる

Honorific "do for me/us": someone of higher status does a favor.

〜てくれるBunpro ↗

Someone does something for the speaker's side. Can be sarcastic (よけいなことをしてくれた).

〜てくれBunpro ↗

Plain, masculine request: "do … for me".

〜ておくれ

Folksy, gentle request (= 〜てください).

〜てあげるBunpro ↗

Do something for someone else's benefit.

〜てやる

Do something for (or to) someone of lower status; also a threat or boast.

〜てみせるBunpro ↗

"I'll … , you'll see": strong resolve to accomplish it.

〜てしまう／〜てしもうたBunpro ↗

Done completely, often with regret.

〜ちまう／〜じまうBunpro ↗

Rough contraction of 〜てしまう／〜でしまう.

〜ておくBunpro ↗

Do in advance, or leave in that state.

〜とくBunpro ↗

Contraction of 〜ておく (おぼえとけ = 覚えておけ).

お + stem + する／いたすBunpro ↗

Humble form: the speaker does something for a superior (お連れする, お会いいたす).

humble verbs

Humble speech for one's own actions: 申す (say), 参る (go/come), いたす (do), 存じる (think).

〜でございます／〜にございますBunpro ↗

Very polite です.

なさるBunpro ↗

Honorific する. 心配なさらないで = "please don't worry".

〜なさいBunpro ↗

Gentle command, from someone in a caring or superior position.

〜ないでBunpro ↗

"Please don't …" (request, short for ないでください).

dictionary form + なBunpro ↗

Prohibition: "don't …" (するな).

〜んじゃない／〜んじゃねえBunpro ↗

"Don't you …": a rough prohibition.

〜た、〜た

Repeated past form as a brusque order: 帰った、帰った = "go on, off home with you".

〜て (request)Bunpro ↗

Bare て-form as a casual request: 逃げて = "run!".

stem + やがる

Shows contempt or anger at someone's action.

stem + おる／おって

Old-style way to speak scornfully of another's action (図に乗りおって).

〜ねえ (= ない)

Rough masculine ない／ai → ee: わかんねえ, うるせえ, すまねえ.

じゃ／じゃろう

Old-man speech: じゃ = だ, じゃろう = だろう, のう = ね. Goes with わし.

〜らしいBunpro ↗

"Apparently": based on what one has heard or seen.

stem + そうだBunpro ↗

"Looks like": よさそう = "looks good", ありそう = "seems there is".

stem + そうな

"The kind of thing (someone) would …": 考えそうなこと.

〜かしらBunpro ↗

"I wonder" (feminine).

〜かなBunpro ↗

"I wonder".

〜ず／〜ずにBunpro ↗

"Without doing" (= ないで). 殺さず = "without killing".

〜たりBunpro ↗

"Do things like …".

〜だけBunpro ↗

"Only, just".

〜しかないBunpro ↗

"Have no choice but to …".

〜間に

"While …": within that span of time.

〜でもBunpro ↗

"Even (just)": すこしでも = "even a little".

いくら〜でもBunpro ↗

"No matter how …; even …".

〜まで (even)

"Even": extends to an unexpected case.

〜までBunpro ↗

"Until".

〜までだ

"All that's left is to …; I'll simply …".

〜わけにはいかないBunpro ↗

"Can't very well …": circumstances or duty don't allow it.

〜としてBunpro ↗

"As (in the role of)".

〜にとってBunpro ↗

"For, from the standpoint of".

〜とはいえBunpro ↗

"Even though they are …; that said".

〜とはBunpro ↗

Surprise at the fact just stated: "to think that …".

〜たあとBunpro ↗

"After doing".

〜ときBunpro ↗

"When".

〜くらいBunpro ↗

Extent: "to the degree that …", "about this much".

〜ほどBunpro ↗

Extent: "so … that", "to the point of".

stem + すぎるBunpro ↗

"Too much".

〜ないようにBunpro ↗

"So that … not": take care that it doesn't happen.

〜てよかったBunpro ↗

"I'm glad that …".

〜て／〜で (reason)

て-form giving a cause or feeling: 心配で = "I was so worried".

〜だろうと思う

"I thought it was probably …".

〜と思うBunpro ↗

"I think that …".

〜じゃないか／〜じゃねえかBunpro ↗

"Isn't it …!": surprise, pointing out, or mild conjecture (んじゃないかな).

〜ですって

Repeating someone's words in disbelief: "…, you say!?".

〜ものか／〜もんですかBunpro ↗

Rhetorical "as if I would …!": strong denial.

〜ものだBunpro ↗

Reflective "that's how it is": a general truth or feeling.

〜というものだBunpro ↗

"That's what you'd call …".

〜ないとBunpro ↗

"Have to …" (short for ないといけない).

〜なきゃBunpro ↗

Contraction of なければ: "unless; have to".

〜ねばならぬ

Old-style "must" (= なければならない).

〜しBunpro ↗

Lists a reason: "and besides …".

〜からBunpro ↗

Reason: "because, so".

〜のでBunpro ↗

Reason, softer than から.

〜ゆえ

Formal, old-fashioned "because, therefore" (= から).

〜のにBunpro ↗

"Even though" (with regret or complaint).

〜なんざ

Rough なんか／など: belittles what comes before.

〜なんてBunpro ↗

"Something like": surprise or belittling.

〜たことがある／ないBunpro ↗

Experience: "have (never) done".

〜つもりBunpro ↗

Intention: "mean to …".

〜ようとするBunpro ↗

"Try to …".

〜ため(に)Bunpro ↗

Purpose "in order to", or "for the sake of".

〜のは〜だ

Emphasis by splitting: "the one who … is …".

〜だけのことはある

"No wonder; that's what you'd expect from …".

〜さえ〜ば

"If only …; as long as …".

〜させてください

"Please let me …" (causative + request).

〜させていただく

"Be allowed to …": very humble way to ask or announce one's action.

〜ちゃ／〜てはBunpro ↗

Contraction of ては: "if (that happens, it's bad)".

〜といってはなんですが

Set phrase: "it's not much of a …, but".

〜といってもBunpro ↗

"Though I say so …".

stem + たいBunpro ↗

"Want to".

stem + おわる

"Finish doing".

〜かもしれない／〜かもしれぬBunpro ↗

"Might".

〜ということBunpro ↗

"The fact that …".

〜ことか

Exclamation: "how (much) …!".

question + か

Embedded question: 何がおこったか = "what happened".

〜たいところだが

"I'd like to …, but".

〜くば

Classical conditional of adjectives: おしくば = 惜しければ "if you value".

〜中Bunpro ↗

Noun + 中 (ちゅう): "during, in the midst of, in the process of".

〜をもってBunpro ↗

Formal "by means of, with" (= で, but formal register).

〜によって／〜によるBunpro ↗

"By means of; due to; depending on"; also marks the agent of a passive.

〜よりBunpro ↗

Comparison "more than"; also a formal "from" (a source, e.g. グランベル皇帝より).

〜てみるBunpro ↗

"To try doing", to do something and see how it goes.

〜わけがないBunpro ↗

"There's no way that …", strong denial.

〜ことになるBunpro ↗

"It turns out that …; it has been decided that …".

〜次第Bunpro ↗

Noun + 次第(で): "depending on …". (Stem + 次第 also means "as soon as …".)

〜なくもないBunpro ↗

"It's not that … won't; not necessarily un-".

verb stem + やすいBunpro ↗

"Easy to …".

verb stem + かける／かけBunpro ↗

〜ばかりBunpro ↗

"Just, only, nothing but".

verb stem + 続けるBunpro ↗

"To continue doing".

〜てからBunpro ↗

"After doing, since doing" (emphasizes A enabling B).

verb stem + な

Casual command: "Do …!" (not the dictionary-form+な prohibition — see na-prohib).

〜ことだBunpro ↗

Firm, authoritative advice: "the thing to do is …; you should …".

〜以上(は)Bunpro ↗

"Since …, now that … (B follows necessarily from A)", formal.

〜によらず

"Regardless of, without regard to".

〜はずだBunpro ↗

"Should be, is expected to be" (confident assumption).

〜からといってBunpro ↗

"Just because … (doesn't mean)".

〜や〜Bunpro ↗

"And" when listing a non-exhaustive sample of nouns (unlike と, a complete list).

〜おる

Dialectal/old-fashioned "to be, to exist" (= いる). Not to be confused with 〜ておる (= teoru).

〜ごと

"Whole, including": 街ごと焼く = "burn the whole city, buildings and all".

〜もののBunpro ↗

"Although, even though" (A is true, but B doesn't follow as expected).

〜てはじめてBunpro ↗

"Only after doing …; not until …".

〜てもらうBunpro ↗

"To have/get someone do something" (for the speaker's benefit).

〜なければならないBunpro ↗

"Must, have to": a double negative — if (A) is not done, it cannot stand.

〜なくてBunpro ↗

Negative て-form; with verbs it usually gives a reason or cause (わからなくて = "I don't understand, and so...").

〜もんBunpro ↗

Casual, often childish/pouty contraction of もの as a sentence-ending reason marker.

〜から (starting point)Bunpro ↗

"From": marks the starting point in time or place — the base meaning of から.

〜まいBunpro ↗

Negative volitional: "won't, shall not", or conjecture "probably won't". Slightly formal/old-fashioned.

〜にしか〜ない

Restrictive: "only... (nobody/nothing else)".

〜ことができるBunpro ↗

"To be able to do": a more formal/written equivalent of the potential form られる.

〜どころかBunpro ↗

"Far from …; let alone": strongly negates what precedes while emphasizing what follows.

〜てほしいBunpro ↗

"Want (someone else) to do" — compare たい (want to do oneself).

verb dictionary form + なりBunpro ↗

"As soon as …": the moment A happens, B follows — often something surprising or driven by strong will. Formal/literary.

〜にするBunpro ↗

"To decide on, to make it (A)": the speaker chooses a specific outcome.

〜わけではないBunpro ↗

"It's not that …; that doesn't mean …": a softened denial.

〜みたいBunpro ↗

〜かどうかBunpro ↗

"Whether or not …".

〜ことにするBunpro ↗

"To decide to …".

〜前にBunpro ↗

"Before doing …".

〜けどBunpro ↗

Casual "but" (= が, けれども).

〜ままBunpro ↗

"Left as is; while remaining in that state".

〜くせにBunpro ↗

"Even though, and yet": despite, with an edge of criticism or scorn (compare the more neutral のに).

〜といえばBunpro ↗

"Speaking of …".

〜ところでBunpro ↗

〜しか〜ないBunpro ↗

"Only, nothing but": restrictive, always with a negative. Stronger than だけ.

"To be in the process of …; gradually continuing/changing". More formal than ている, used for ongoing change (increase, decrease, decline) rather than simple physical motion.

〜にちがいない／〜にちがいありませんBunpro ↗

"Must not, cannot": formal/written prohibition, stronger than てはいけない.

〜(た)方がいいBunpro ↗

〜なり〜なりBunpro ↗

〜わけだBunpro ↗

〜とて

〜ざるを得ないBunpro ↗

"Can't help doing; have no choice but to", often said reluctantly.

〜までもないBunpro ↗

"There's no need to even …": too obvious or minor to be worth it.

〜であるBunpro ↗

Literary/formal copula, = だ. Can also replace な／の linking one noun to another.

verb dictionary form + べくBunpro ↗

Formal/literary "in order to, so as to": B is an intentional action aimed at achieving A.

"A person in the position of A; as befits A". Classical, from たり (と＋あり).

adjective stem + かろうBunpro ↗

Classical conjecture for i-adjectives (= だろう): 高かろう = "is probably expensive". Old-fashioned, used by the elderly or the authoritative.

お + stem + になるBunpro ↗

Honorific "to do": respect for someone else's action, never the speaker's own. する verbs use ご〜になる; いく／くる／いる become おいでになる.

〜てならないBunpro ↗

"Can't help feeling …": used with feelings and emotions.

〜と (conditional)Bunpro ↗

Verb/adjective + と: an automatic or habitual result, stronger than ば／なら／たら.

verb dictionary form + べきBunpro ↗

〜上にBunpro ↗

Formal "in addition to, besides, on top of": adds a second fact of the same polarity.

〜ないでもないBunpro ↗

Double negative: "it's not that … isn't the case" — a hedged, tentative positive.

〜限りBunpro ↗

〜としたら

〜とやら

〜としてもBunpro ↗
