それは　また　別べつの物語ものがたりである・・・And so, the great upheaval that would later come to be called the "Bern Uprising," one that had engulfed the entire continent, drew to a close. The kingdom of Bern surrendered to the kingdom of Etruria along with the death of King Zephiel. The kingdom of Etruria dissolved the kingdom of Bern once, and, citing the distance involved, did not rule it directly, but instead had a new kingdom of Bern founded anew on Bern's land, with Guinevere as its queen. There remained deep-rooted backlash from Bern's former vassals and citizens over handing the "Fire Emblem" to the enemy, but as a result of Guinevere's persistent efforts, the newborn kingdom of Bern, though one couldn't call its progress smooth, pressed on down the road to recovery. The whereabouts of Idunn, the "Priestess of Darkness" who had served close by Zephiel's side, were never discovered, and the matter ended there. The world, under the leadership of the kingdom of Etruria, once again regained stability and peace. In time, the continent of Elibe, in an age far later than that of Roy and the others, would come to be united into a single nation—but that is another story entirely...
