# A point with no URL isn't in Bunpro's catalog (dialect, classical, or very colloquial).
tsutsu|〜つつ|Literary "while doing", simultaneous action. 保ちつつ = "while maintaining".|
ni-kawatte|〜に代わって|"In place of, on behalf of".|
to-iu|〜という|"It is said that, called". Reports what's heard or named.|
beshi|〜べし|Classical auxiliary: "should, must". 罰するべし = "should be punished".|
wake-niwa-ikanai|〜わけにはいかない|"Cannot possibly ~" - a social/moral obligation makes the action impossible.|
no-dearu|〜のである|Formal, written explanatory sentence ending, = 〜のだ.|
toshite|〜として|"As, in the capacity of".|
kurai|〜くらい／〜ぐらい|"About, to the extent of".|
sasete-morau|〜させてもらう|"Let me do ~" - asking/stating permission to do something for one's own benefit.|
rashii|〜らしい|"Seems like, I heard that" - inference from outside information.|
sae-ba|〜さえ〜ば|"As long as ~, if only ~".|
tehoshii|〜てほしい|"Want (someone) to do ~".|
tewa-naranu|〜てはならぬ／〜てはならない|"Must not do ~".|
yagaru|〜やがる|Contemptuous auxiliary added to a verb stem - "have the nerve to ~".|
ni-kaete|〜に代えても|"Even at the cost of ~". 命にかえても = "even at the cost of my life".|
ijou|〜以上|"Given that ~ (as a natural consequence)".|
nakerebanaranai|〜なければならない|"Must do ~".|
nasai|〜なさい|Polite command form, softer than the plain imperative.|
koto-ga-dekinai|〜ことができない|"Cannot do ~".|
shika-nai|〜しかない|"Nothing but ~, only ~".|
ni-tsuki|〜につき|"Per, for each".|
to-yoi|〜とよい|"It's good to ~, you should ~".|
nikui|〜にくい|"Hard to do ~".|
goto-ni|〜ごとに|"Each time, every ~".|
teshimau|〜てしまう|"End up doing ~, finish doing ~" - often with a sense of regret or finality.|
nioite|〜において|"In, at, with regard to" - formal.|
senebanaranu|〜せねばならぬ|Classical "must do ~", = 〜しなければならない.|
koso|〜こそ|Emphatic particle: "it is precisely ~, ~ of all things".|
shiru-yoshi-mo-nai|知る由もない|Idiom: "to have no way of knowing".|
niyotte|〜によって|"By, due to" - marks the agent or cause, especially in passive sentences.|
de-aru|〜である|Formal written copula, = だ／です.|
teiru|〜ている|Ongoing action or resulting state ("is doing", "has done"). Past: 〜ていた.|
totomoni|〜とともに|"Together with".|
renyou|verb stem, … (連用中止)|Ending a clause on the verb's plain stem links it to the next clause, like "and" - a formal, written alternative to the て-form.|
passive|〜れる／〜られる|Passive: "be …-ed". The doer is marked with に.|
de-copula|〜で (copula て-form)|で here is the て-form of だ: "it is … and".|
zu-neg|〜ず|Classical negative, = 〜ない. Often links to the next clause like a て-form.|
tekudasai|〜てください|Polite request, "please do ~".|
kono-mama-dewa|このままでは|"If things stay as they are".|
te-orenai|〜ておれません|Dialect/classical "cannot remain ~ing", = 〜ていられない.|
kamoshirenai|〜かもしれない|"Might be, perhaps" - expresses a possibility.|
kanenai|〜かねない|"Might well do ~, cannot rule out ~" - a negative possibility.|
