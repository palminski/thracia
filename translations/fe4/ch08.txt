1	The Thracia Peninsula has two faces.
2	The northern half is a land of rich grassy plains, divided among many small kingdoms,
3	while the southern half, among its harsh mountains and barren earth, holds the kingdom of Thracia, known for its dragon knights.
4	Thracia, known for its reckless valor,
5	had repeatedly invaded in the past to seize the rich northern lands,
6	but had failed again and again against the resistance of the Leonster royal family.
7	It seemed that annihilating Leonster's army in the Yied Desert had finally achieved that long-held ambition,
8	but then Prince Bloom of House Friege conquered this region with his great host,
9	and ever since, it had remained under the Empire's control as the Kingdom of North Thracia.
10	From then, for over a decade, even the ambitious King Travant
11	found himself utterly unable to act against the Empire's military might,
12	and a strange peace was maintained.
13	But the Liberation Army's capture of the capital, Alster,
14	upset the delicate balance of this region.
15	Alster Castle and Leonster Castle were now held by Celice's Liberation Army,
16	while at Conote Castle, King Bloom, having fled from Alster, gathered troops,
17	and at Manster Castle, Princess Ishtar, known by the epithet Thunder God,
18	stood firm in her defenses against Thracia,
19	while in Thracian-held Miloah, King Travant,
20	together with his front-line commander General Mycoff, kept an uncanny silence.
21	The people's one and only wish was
22	that this land would once again return to the rule of the Leonster royal family.
23	And now, the curtain rose on a new battle.
